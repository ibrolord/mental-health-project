MHtoolkit is a free, private mental health companion that gives you clinical-grade self-assessment tools, AI-powered conversation therapy, mood tracking, and daily habit-building features — all in one app. No subscriptions. No ads. No data sold. Ever.

MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT SHOULD BE ACCESSIBLE TO EVERYONE

Whether you're between therapy sessions, on a waitlist, managing day-to-day stress, or simply want better tools for self-awareness, MHtoolkit meets you where you are.

AI CHAT THERAPIST
Have a conversation with an AI companion that remembers your mood patterns, assessment history, and personal context. Voice therapy mode lets you speak naturally and hear responses aloud.

VALIDATED MENTAL HEALTH ASSESSMENTS
Track your mental health over time with clinician-grade screening tools:
- PHQ-9 — Depression severity screening
- GAD-7 — Generalized anxiety assessment
- PSS-4 — Perceived stress scale
- CBI — Copenhagen Burnout Inventory

MOOD TRACKING
Log your mood in seconds with emoji-based check-ins. View trend charts that reveal patterns you might not notice on your own.

GOAL SETTING THAT ACTUALLY WORKS
Choose from five proven frameworks: Eisenhower Matrix, Ivy Lee Method, 1-3-5 Rule, ABCDE Method, or free-form goals.

HABIT TRACKING WITH STREAKS
Build daily practices that support your mental health. Track habits and watch your consistency grow.

PERSONALIZED DAILY AFFIRMATIONS
AI-generated affirmations drawn from your actual mood data, assessment results, and goals.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
- Use anonymously — no account required
- Export all your data anytime
- Delete everything with one tap
- No data sold to third parties

MHtoolkit is not a replacement for professional care. It's a free daily toolkit that helps you understand yourself better. If you're in crisis, MHtoolkit connects you with emergency resources including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
