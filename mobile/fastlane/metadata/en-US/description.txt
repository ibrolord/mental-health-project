MHtoolkit is a free, private mental health companion with published-source self-assessment screeners, AI-powered conversation support, mood tracking, and daily habit-building features - all in one app. No subscriptions. No ads. No data sold.

Medical disclaimer: MHtoolkit does not provide medical diagnoses or treatment advice. Seek a doctor's advice in addition to using this app and before making medical decisions, especially if you have symptoms, safety concerns, or plan to change care.

MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT SHOULD BE ACCESSIBLE TO EVERYONE

Whether you're between care options, on a waitlist, managing day-to-day stress, or simply want better tools for self-awareness, MHtoolkit meets you where you are.

AI CHAT COMPANION
Have a conversation with an AI companion that can use your mood patterns, assessment history, and personal context when you choose personalized responses. The app asks for consent before sending chat text, voice audio/transcripts, or personalized context to third-party AI providers. Voice support mode lets you speak naturally and hear responses aloud.

PUBLISHED-SOURCE SELF-ASSESSMENTS
Track symptom and exhaustion patterns with published self-report tools. The app shows the recall period, scoring limitations, and source citation before and after each tool:
- PHQ-9 - Depression symptom screener
- GAD-7 - Anxiety symptom screener
- CBI - Personal burnout self-report measure

MOOD TRACKING
Log your mood in seconds with emoji-based check-ins. View trend charts that reveal patterns you might not notice on your own.

GOAL SETTING THAT ACTUALLY WORKS
Choose from five proven frameworks: Eisenhower Matrix, Ivy Lee Method, 1-3-5 Rule, ABCDE Method, or free-form goals.

HABIT TRACKING WITH STREAKS
Build daily practices that support your mental health. Track habits and watch your consistency grow.

PERSONALIZED DAILY AFFIRMATIONS
AI-generated affirmations drawn from your actual mood data, assessment results, and goals.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
- Use anonymously — no account required
- Export all your data anytime
- Delete everything with one tap
- No data sold to third parties
- AI features ask before sharing selected data with AI providers for processing

MHtoolkit is not a replacement for professional care, diagnosis, or treatment. Seek advice from a doctor or licensed mental health professional in addition to using MHtoolkit and before making medical decisions. A PHQ-9 response about thoughts of death or self-harm displays immediate safety guidance and 988 contact options for the U.S. and Canada.
