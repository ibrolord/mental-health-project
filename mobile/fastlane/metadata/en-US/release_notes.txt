What's new in 1.0.9:

- Safer support when things feel heavy. If you write something worrying, the app now shows call and text options right away, including in more languages and with common misspellings.
- Outside Canada or the US? There's now a quick link to find a helpline in your country.
- The Advisor is more careful. Goals like stopping medication now get a safety card instead of a quick tip.
- Chat fixes: long conversations keep working, and error messages no longer end up in your chat.
