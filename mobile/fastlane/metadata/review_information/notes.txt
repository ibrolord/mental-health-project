SUGGESTED REVIEW PATH
1. Launch and continue anonymously.
2. Save a mood check-in, return to Today, and reopen Mood to confirm history.
3. Open Assess. PHQ-9, GAD-7, and CBI show recall periods, limitations, non-diagnostic wording, and published sources. A positive PHQ-9 self-harm response immediately shows safety guidance and crisis actions.
4. Add a goal and habit. Goals support milestones, due dates, notes, reminders, attachments, and Focus handoff. Duplicate active entries and repeated taps are guarded.
5. Review Library, Journal, Life Planner, Focus, Meditation, Ground Me, Mind Games, routines, and affirmations.
6. Open AI Chat. Sending first displays AI data-sharing consent. Personal context is off by default and selectable by category. Explicit crisis language receives a deterministic safety response.
7. Open Settings to revoke AI consent, export or delete data, delete a permanent account, contact support, and open crisis resources.
8. On iPhone, optionally set up read-only Apple Health access. Mood shows permitted sleep, movement, workout, mindfulness, and State of Mind summaries. Raw HealthKit samples remain on device. An aggregate can be sent to AI only after a per-request preview and confirmation.

MEDICAL AND PRIVACY BOUNDARIES
MHtoolkit supports self-management and reflection. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a doctor or licensed professional. Partners receive only user-enabled activity counts and fixed-format celebrations; database authorization prevents access to raw private content. AI consent can be revoked in Settings.

iPAD COMPATIBILITY
The app is iPhone-only and requires fullscreen, and runs in iPhone compatibility mode on iPad. Build 59 uses expo-notifications 0.32.17 with Expo's iOS serializer thread-safety fix and omits expo-device. The signed IPA was verified as iPhone-only and includes the notification native module.
