# Quiet Momentum artwork

Selected visual: `selected-quiet-momentum.png`, the first displayed Image Gen concept.
Selection delegated by the user. Built-in Image Gen was used, not a CLI fallback.
All artwork is generated illustration, not a photo of a real user or testimonial.

## Consumed assets

- `mobile/assets/welcome-steady.png`: pausing by a sunlit window with a mug.
- `mobile/assets/welcome-routine.png`: writing one small routine in a notebook.
- `mobile/assets/welcome-goals.png`: arranging a simple next step on a board.

All three are RGBA PNGs, approximately 2.4-2.5 MiB each. Original generated bytes
were copied into the repository without cropping, stretching, or recompositing.
Consumer: `mobile/components/WelcomeArtwork.tsx`. Bundled local require calls
avoid network fetching and permit offline display. No font or library added.

## Production prompt set

The selected concept was supplied as the visual reference for the first asset.
The generated first asset was supplied as reference for the other two.

Steady: Generate the standalone production illustration from the selected MHtoolkit
concept, wide 1400x900. Same young Black adult woman with curly hair in a loose bun,
forest green sweater and cream trousers, sitting comfortably on a sage sofa by a
sunlit window, holding an ivory mug in both hands; open unlettered notebook and pen
nearby. Premium gouache/cut-paper rendering, cream and pale ochre light, sage
plants, terracotta cushion, dry-brush paper texture. Natural anatomy, calm candid
expression, no meditation pose. Feathered irregular painted edges to transparent
alpha. Keep head, hands and notebook visible. No text, logo, slogans, UI, buttons,
phone chrome, or watermark. Readable at a small native onboarding hero size.

Routine: Match the same woman, clothes, palette, room and rendering. She sits at a
small pale wooden desk writing a short note in an open unlettered notebook. One
hand writes and the other rests naturally on the page. Ivory mug, terracotta
cushion, plants at the edges. A small everyday routine, not stressed achievement.
Wide scene with transparent painted edges and no words, numbers, UI or watermark.

Goals: Match the same woman, clothes, palette, room and rendering. Seated at the
desk in three-quarter profile, she pins one blank ochre note to a restrained cork
board beside the window. Two other blank cream notes form a short sequence. A
doable next step, not hustling or triumph. Natural hand, head and hands visible,
unlettered notebook and ivory mug. Same landscape density and transparent painted
edges. No words, symbols on notes, UI, logo or watermark.

## Motion intent

The scene changes with the focus. A short crossfade and 2.5% settling scale provide
continuity, not an endless loop or fake loading. The illustration is decorative
and cannot intercept taps or accessibility focus. Reduced or unresolved motion
preference renders one plain static image. Accessibility text sizes remove the
illustration to prioritize the controls.
