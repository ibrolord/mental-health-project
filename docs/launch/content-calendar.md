## Founder Launch Post

I built the check-in I wanted on overloaded days.

MHtoolkit asks one useful question: how are you doing right now?

The check-in takes about 30 seconds. You can use it in the browser without
signup, there are no ads, and you can delete your data. Try it for seven days
and look for patterns rather than chasing a perfect streak.

It is a self-reflection tool, not therapy, diagnosis, or crisis support.

[Founder LinkedIn campaign link]

I am looking for the first 100 adults willing to use it for a week and tell me
what made them return or stop.
