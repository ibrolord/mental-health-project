# A Seven-Day Private Check-In for Your Community

## What MHtoolkit Is

MHtoolkit is a free, private self-reflection tool for adults. A daily check-in
takes about 30 seconds and helps a person notice patterns over seven days.

- Works in a browser with no download required
- No signup required for anonymous use
- No ads or sale of personal information
- Optional notes, goals, habits, assessments, and AI-guided reflection
- Data deletion controls and a public privacy policy

MHtoolkit is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, medical advice, or emergency
support.

## The Pilot

1. A partner reviews the experience and safety language.
2. The partner shares one optional tracked link with a small adult audience.
3. Participants decide independently whether to use it.
4. After 14 days, MHtoolkit reports aggregate activations and repeat-use rates.

There is no fee for the pilot and no requirement for a participant to register.

## What Partners Receive

- A partner-specific link
- A short approved description
- A 20-second screen-recording brief
- Aggregate counts for first check-ins, three-of-seven-day engagement, and
  days 8-14 return

Partners do not receive names, emails, mood entries, notes, assessments, chat
content, or any other user-level wellbeing data.

## What We Ask

- Try the tool before sharing it.
- Preserve the non-clinical and optional-use language.
- Share it once through an approved channel.
- Provide one short feedback call after the pilot.

## Suggested Description

Try a private 30-second check-in for seven days and see whether it helps you
notice patterns. MHtoolkit works in your browser with no signup or ads, and you
can delete your data anytime. It is a self-help reflection tool, not therapy or
emergency support.
